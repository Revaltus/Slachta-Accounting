## Accounting support made for independent agents and agency owners

Commission income, renewal cycles, contingent payouts, producer draws. Agency finances run on a calendar that standard bookkeeping templates were never built for.

Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services has worked with insurance agencies since opening in Stillwater, Minnesota, in 2005. Agents and agency owners across Minnesota and Wisconsin, plus clients nationwide, come to us because we understand the lag between writing a policy and seeing that revenue land in the bank account.

That knowledge shapes how we set up your books. You see cash position and profitability by line of business, and you get insight you can act on, not a report on where you were last quarter. This holds whether you run a two-person agency near Stillwater or a multi-location operation with producers across state lines.

## Common financial challenges facing insurance agencies

- **Uneven renewal revenue.** Income follows policy cycles, not calendar quarters. Without current, connected reporting, forecasting cash position becomes guesswork.
- **Expense creep.** E&O premiums, licensing fees, CRM and quoting software, and marketing spend rarely get tracked against production as it happens.
- **Growth budgeting.** Hiring a producer or opening a second location is hard to plan without a clear view of next quarter's cash position.
- **Late contingent commissions.** Contingent and override commissions arrive months after the policy was written, so cash flow planning has to be proactive instead of reactive catch-up.

## How we help insurance agencies

### Bookkeeping built for commission revenue
icon: Receipt

We reconcile commission statements against carrier deposits and categorize income by line of business. Your books reflect how the agency really earns, and you always have a current, accurate picture.

### Financial insights by book of business
icon: ChartBar

Monthly financials broken out by product line and producer show which parts of the agency are growing and which are quietly draining resources. Small issues get caught before they become costly ones.

### KPI tracking for agencies
icon: Target

We track retention rate, revenue per producer, and expense ratio, and bring those numbers to you proactively. Staffing and growth decisions rest on data, not instinct.

### Payroll for staff and producers
icon: Users

Salaried support staff, hourly CSRs, and producers on commission splits or draws all get accurate, compliant payroll runs. Withholding, filings, and year-end reporting happen on schedule, and we update the setup when producers join or leave mid-year.

icon: Calculator

We plan your agency's tax position all year, covering entity structure, quarterly estimates, and timing of income and expenses. We also evaluate SEP IRA, SIMPLE IRA, and 401(k) options and coordinate with your financial advisor so planning lines up.

## Common questions from insurance agency owners

**Q: Do you work with insurance agencies outside of Minnesota?**
A: Yes. Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services is based in Stillwater, Minnesota, and serves agencies nationwide, with a concentration of clients in Minnesota and Wisconsin. Outsourced accounting, payroll, and tax services are delivered remotely on a secure platform, so your location is no barrier to working together or to getting real-time financial visibility.

**Q: How does accounting for an insurance agency differ from a typical small business?**
A: Agencies earn revenue through commissions, renewals, and contingent payouts that arrive on carrier schedules rather than fixed billing cycles. Books need to track profitability by line of business and producer, not just total revenue. Ongoing reporting gives owners visibility to make proactive, data-driven decisions during the year instead of relying on a historical snapshot.

**Q: Can you handle payroll for producers paid on commission?**
A: Yes. We process payroll for salaried and hourly staff alongside producers paid through commission splits, draws, and true-ups. Withholding, filings, and year-end reporting are handled consistently across both compensation types, and we update the setup when producers are added or leave mid-year, so nothing is reconstructed at tax time.

**Q: Do you coordinate with our existing financial advisor?**
A: Yes. We collaborate with agency owners' financial advisors on retirement plan evaluation, whether that is a SEP IRA, SIMPLE IRA, or 401(k). Tax planning and personal financial planning move together, which matters most when you are deciding how much to contribute in a strong commission year.

**Q: What does outsourced accounting look like for an insurance agency?**
A: It pulls commission data, carrier deposits, and operating expenses into one place, giving you visibility into cash position and profitability by line of business. We close your books monthly, so instead of waiting for a year-end report you get ongoing insight and proactive guidance, without the cost of a full-time controller.

**Q: How does entity structure affect my agency's taxes?**
A: Whether your agency is an S corp, LLC, or another structure affects self-employment tax, how owner compensation is handled, and which deductions are available. We review that structure as your agency grows, not just at formation, alongside quarterly estimates and the timing of income and expenses.

**Q: Can retirement plans lower my tax bill as an agency owner?**
A: They can. Contributions to a SEP IRA, SIMPLE IRA, or 401(k) can meaningfully reduce an owner's tax burden while building long-term savings. We evaluate these options as part of year-round tax planning, so your return reflects choices made months earlier rather than last-minute adjustments.

**Q: Can you help if I am planning an exit, partner buyout, or semi-retirement?**
A: Yes. Established agency owners weighing an exit, a partner buyout, or a move toward semi-retirement need retirement plan evaluation, cash flow projections, and personal tax planning to move together. We coordinate those pieces with your financial advisor so they are not handled in separate conversations.
